# Third party content

## Dungeon room and secret data

`src/main/resources/assets/pawfectaddons/dungeons/` contains dungeon room skeletons and secret
waypoint locations derived from [DungeonRoomsMod](https://github.com/Quantizr/DungeonRoomsMod)
and from [Skyblocker](https://github.com/SkyblockerMod/Skyblocker).
