Route sets bundled with PawfectAddons

default.json
  The Secret Routes Mod's routes (3ppopka's set), by yourboykyle, zyra and the Secret Routes team.
  https://github.com/yourboykyle/SecretRoutes (3ppopkaroutes.json), GPL-3.0.

flameofwar.json
  FlameOfWar's routes from the Secret Routes Mod, by FlameOfWar, Itplays, wyannnnn and yourboykyle.
  https://github.com/yourboykyle/SecretRoutes (fowroutes.json), GPL-3.0.

stella.json
  Stella's default routes, recorded by Luckkytigger for Stella by Eclipse (NEXD_).
  https://github.com/Eclipse-5214/ether (routes/default.json), LGPL-3.0.

pawfect.json
  PawfectAddons' own routes for rooms the community routes get wrong. Some are built on
  Stella's routes (LGPL-3.0); the rest are from in-game waypoints.
